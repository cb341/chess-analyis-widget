A Static PGN Replay

This page is only HTML, CSS, JavaScript, assets, and a PGN file. The widget reads SAN, comments, clocks, and lichess eval annotations in the browser.

Supported PGN Features

  • Tag pairs, including player names, result, ratings, and site data
  • Custom starts with `[SetUp "1"]` and `[FEN "..."]`
  • SAN moves for pieces and pawns
  • Captures, castling, promotion, and en passant
  • Disambiguation such as `Nbd7` and `R1e2`
  • Check and checkmate suffixes
  • Comments attached to each ply
  • Lichess `[%eval]` and `[%clk]` annotations
  • Symbolic and numeric NAG glyphs
  • Main-line replay with variations skipped
