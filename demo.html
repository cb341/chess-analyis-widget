A Static PGN Replay

This page is only HTML, CSS, JavaScript, assets, and a PGN file. The widget reads SAN, comments, clocks, and lichess eval annotations in the browser.

The opening follows a normal Ruy Lopez. The comments shown under the board come directly from PGN comment blocks, while the board state is rebuilt from SAN moves.

In the middle game, White uses the center and queenside structure to reduce Black's counterplay. The bounds keep this board focused on the paragraph's moves.

The final tactic is shown as mate scores because lichess exports mate evals as `[%eval #n]`. No engine runs here.
